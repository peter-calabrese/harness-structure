## Engineering Baseline

- Follow `standards/engineering.md`, `standards/architecture.md`, `standards/security.md`, and `standards/testing.md`.
- **Frontend (React + TypeScript)**: Follow `standards/accessibility.md` and `standards/frontend/*`.
- **Backend (Java + Spring Boot)**: Follow `standards/backend/*` (constructor injection, Java `record` DTOs, Jakarta `@Valid`, RFC 9457 `ProblemDetail`, Spring Security, SLF4J + MDC).
- **DevOps & Data**: Follow `standards/devops/*` and `standards/data/*`.
- Route or delegate specialized tasks to the agents in `agents/` (`orchestrator`, `ui`, `backend`, `devops`, `data`, `code-reviewer`, `security-reviewer`).
